# Presentation

[When_Should_AI_Assist_the_Assessor_FINAL.pptx](When_Should_AI_Assist_the_Assessor_FINAL.pptx) is the
seven-minute talk (slides 1 to 23) with an appendix for questions; the PDF is a viewing copy.

Every slide is visible in this copy, and the PDF shows all 42 with a navigation outline, so a reader can jump to any slide by its title from the left-hand pane. Four slides (7, 8, 10 and 20) were skipped in
the live talk for time; they are unhidden here so the deck reads on its own.

The order follows the version presented on September 19, 2026: how one case was built (slide 11) and the
exploratory extensions (slides 15 to 17) are in the talk, while the method sources, the software checks and
the one-case walk-through are in the appendix (A4 to A6). The numbers and claims match the paper, including
the answers the checks withheld, the reviewer and AI-judge agreement, and the open-weight and
whole-document collections. Reviewers are named only in the author's own copy, never here.
