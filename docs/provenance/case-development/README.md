# Completed case-development evidence

The 24 case folders collect completed construction and review records. Start with each case's README; the second-review summary is edited for readability, while its event JSON preserves the researcher's actual wording and timing.

| Stage | Included evidence |
|---|---|
| Initial construction | 24 prompts, responses and completed initial-review derivatives |
| Revision generation | 23 prepared prompts associated with received outputs, plus raw and parsed replies; W1-03 used direct AI-assisted revision |
| Second human review | 24 exact reviewed candidates, completed event records and editorial summaries |
| Supplementary AI audit | 24 integrated development records, exact audit targets, prompt packets, raw replies, normalized replies and receipt records |

The auditor reported no substantive issue for 21 targets and correction needed for three. Those verdicts describe the archived targets only; they are not independent human validation or final approval. W1-07's extra JSON field and W3-06's trailing prose are preserved in raw replies and receipts.

The files in these folders are fixed historical records. Do not overwrite them with later case versions or treat them as final generator inputs. Reference answers and scoring are present in several files. Final model-visible inputs will be supplied separately after freeze.

The additional model check is a supplementary AI-assisted case audit conducted during development. User-reported model labels are recorded as such; unknown backend, interface and timing details remain unknown. It contributes no experimental observations. Policy excerpts were audited as supplied; the AI audit did not open the original local documents or certify policy currency.

Raw evidence retains its original wording and internal identifiers. Editorial summaries are not verbatim quotations or new researcher judgments. File fingerprints and transformation notes are in [the artifact manifest](../artifact-manifest_2026-09-13.json). Academic calendars and policy snapshots are listed with URLs and SHA-256 in [literature/institutional-sources.csv](../../../literature/institutional-sources.csv); full copies are not redistributed.
